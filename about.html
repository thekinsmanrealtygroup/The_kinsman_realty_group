The Rental Process

1. Submit Application

Complete our online application form with your personal and employment details.

2. Pay Application Fee

$95 per adult for credit/background checks and processing.

3. Schedule a Tour

View the property in person or via virtual tour.

4. Claim Your Home

Sign the lease, pay deposit, and move in!

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How long does the application process take? Typically 2-5 business days after receiving all documents.

❓ What documents do I need? Government ID, proof of income (pay stubs/tax returns), and rental history.

❓ Is the application fee refundable? No, it's non-refundable and covers credit/background checks.

❓ Can I apply with roommates? Yes! Each adult occupant must submit an application and pay the $95 fee.

❓ What credit score do I need? Minimum 620 recommended, but we consider all applications.
